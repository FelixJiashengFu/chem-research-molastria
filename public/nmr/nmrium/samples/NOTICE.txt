Sample NMR data for the NMRium page (public/nmr/nmrium/). Measured data, copied unchanged from two npm packages
by cheminfo, both under the MIT licence (texts below). The measurements are those packages' test data.

aspirin-1h.zip         Bruker 1H, raw FID (fid, acqus, pdata)       bruker-data-test 1.1.1  data/zipped/aspirin-1h.zip
cyclosporin-1h.zip     Bruker 1H, raw FID                           bruker-data-test 1.1.1  data/zipped/cyclosporin/cyclosporin_1h.zip
cyclosporin-cosy.zip   Bruker H,H-COSY, raw 2D data (ser)           bruker-data-test 1.1.1  data/zipped/cyclosporin/cyclosporin_cosy.zip
indometacin-1h.dx      JCAMP-DX 1H spectrum, 400 MHz, DMSO-d6       jcamp-data-test 2.6.0   data/nmr/indometacin/1h.dx
indometacin.mol        Molfile of indometacin                       jcamp-data-test 2.6.0   data/nmr/indometacin/structure.mol
